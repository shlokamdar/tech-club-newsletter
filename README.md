## Event 01: Roadmap to Getting Certified

### Beyond the Certificate

The first newsletter covers the Tech Club's **Roadmap to Getting Certified** event, featuring panel members:

* **Harsh**
* **Riya**
* **Naman**

The session explored how students can approach professional certifications and make them part of a broader learning and career journey.

### Topics Covered

* Cybersecurity certifications
* Ethical hacking and penetration testing
* Cloud certifications
* AI and AI security
* Certification preparation
* Proctored examinations
* Practical and hands-on learning
* CTFs and hackathons
* Building projects alongside certifications
* Choosing a technology domain
* Starting certification preparation early
* University and certification opportunities

The panel also discussed the realities of certification examinations, including the **six-hour CEH examination** and the **24-hour CPENT examination, divided into two 12-hour slots**.

## About the Deakin Tech Club

The **Deakin Tech Club** is a student community focused on technology, learning, collaboration and professional development.

The club provides opportunities for students to:

* Explore emerging technologies
* Learn from industry and student experiences
* Develop practical technical skills
* Discover technology career pathways
* Learn about professional certifications
* Participate in technical workshops and activities
* Connect and collaborate with fellow students
